Full User Control & Data Sovereignty

User Privacy Choices

Effective Date: October 2026

At DeskStacks, we believe that privacy is not just a policy—it is an architectural guarantee. Because DeskStacks does not collect, transmit, or monetize your personal information, you are always in complete control.

Your Controls & Rights

1. Zero Data Selling & Zero Tracking

DeskStacks does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or European GDPR. There is no tracking code to opt out of because none exists in our binary.

2. Managing macOS Permissions

You can grant or revoke DeskStacks permissions (Files & Folders, Reminders, Calendar, Speech Recognition) at any moment through macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security.

3. Instant Data Erasure

All user preferences, stack configurations, and scratchpad notes are stored locally in macOS application preferences. To delete all local DeskStacks data, simply reset settings inside the app or delete the app support container.

Direct Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions or requests regarding your data sovereignty, contact us at: support@deskstacks.app.
