⌨️ Global Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Option + Space or F7 — Quick Reveal: Instantly brings all active stacks and capsules to the front.
  • Command + Shift + W — Desktop Sweep: Tidies up desktop clutter into organized folders.
  • Command + Shift + F or Option + F — OmniSearch: Opens instant fuzzy search across files, notes, and tasks.
  • Command + Shift + V or Option + V — Smart Voice: Activates hands-free voice dictation for scratchpad notes and reminders.
  • Command + Option + P — Manage License / Paywall: View trial status or manage StoreKit subscriptions.
  • Command + , — Preferences: Opens DeskStacks settings panel.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the 14-day Free Trial work?

Upon first launching DeskStacks, you receive an unrestricted 14-day trial with full access to all features, custom folder stacks, reminders studio, and desktop sweep. You can unlock lifetime access or choose an affordable subscription anytime.

Does DeskStacks upload any of my personal files or notes?

Never. DeskStacks operates with 100% on-device architecture. All file scans, Markdown notes, reminders, and audio transcriptions are processed strictly on your Mac using native Apple frameworks. Zero data is sent to external servers.

What is the difference between Desktop Pinned and Floating HUD mode?

In Desktop Pinned mode, stacks sit directly above your desktop wallpaper behind other windows, creating a persistent, interactive workspace. In Floating HUD mode, stacks stay above your open application windows for rapid drag-and-drop staging.

How do I restore my purchase on a new Mac?

Open DeskStacks, press Command + Option + P (or select "Unlock DeskStacks" from the menu bar icon), and click "Restore Purchases". StoreKit 2 will automatically verify your license via your Apple ID.
